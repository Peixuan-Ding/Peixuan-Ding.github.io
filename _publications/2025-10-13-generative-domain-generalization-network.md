Abstract:
Changes in equipment operating conditions can shift data distributions and substantially reduce the performance of learned diagnostic models. This work presents an adversarial diffusion augmented network (ADAN) for domain-generalized intelligent fault diagnosis when the target operating condition is unavailable and source-domain data are limited. Classifier-guided diffusion augments the source distribution, while adversarial learning extracts domain-invariant features across multiple source domains. Experiments on two public bearing datasets demonstrate strong diagnostic performance under complex, previously unseen operating conditions.
