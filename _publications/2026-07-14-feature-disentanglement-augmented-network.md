Abstract:
Vibration-based fault diagnosis models often degrade when operating conditions shift beyond the distributions observed during training. This work proposes a feature disentanglement augmented network (FDAN) that explicitly separates domain-invariant and domain-specific representations. An independently trained attention module identifies classification-relevant components for feature augmentation, improving the robustness and generalization of rotating-machinery diagnosis under previously unseen operating conditions.
